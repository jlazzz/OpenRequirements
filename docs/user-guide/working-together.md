# Working together

## Two people editing one item

Your edits are a private draft until you click **Save version**. Meanwhile:

- **Who else is editing.** Above the item's name, "Also editing: Anna (draft saved 10:42)" means Anna has unsaved edits to this item. You cannot see them until she saves.
- **A version saved while you have the item open.** Within 15 seconds a yellow bar says so. Click **Merge it into my edits**, or **Show version N** if you have not changed anything.
- **Saving after someone else.** If you click **Save version** and someone saved first, nothing is lost: the page reloads with your edits and the merge panel.

### The merge panel

It lists what the other person changed since your draft began.

- Changes to different parts (they changed Status, you changed the description) combine by themselves.
- A part you both changed differently is shown side by side. For each, choose **Keep mine** or **Take Anna's**.

**Combine and keep editing** puts the result in your draft. Check it, then **Save version**. **Discard my edits** throws your draft away and shows their version.

Rich text is compared as a whole description: if you both changed the description, you choose one of the two, then copy anything you need from the other by hand. The other version stays under **Versions**.

## Notifications

The bell at the top right shows how many notifications you have not read. You get one when:

- someone mentions you in a comment (`@Your Name`)
- someone invites you to review or approve a review
- someone assigns you test runs
- someone changes an item, and a relationship to an item you saved last becomes suspect

Click a notification to open what it is about; that marks it read. **Mark all read** clears the count. You are never notified of your own actions.

### By email

When the server has email set up, each notification is also emailed. To change that, click your name at the top right, then under **Email me about notifications** choose:

- **Each time**: one email per notification (the default)
- **Daily digest**: at most one email a day, listing what you have not read
- **Never**

## Forgot your password

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** and enter your email. The link in the email works once, for one hour. Setting a new password signs you out everywhere else.

The link appears only when the server can send email. Otherwise ask an administrator: under **Users**, **Reset link** makes a link to send you. It works once, for 3 days.
